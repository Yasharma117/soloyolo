description: Use when building or changing any soloyolo screen or UI (the Expo app in mobile/). Points to the design system package and its rules.
---

The design system lives in `packages/design-system/`. Read its `SKILL.md` and `README.md` and follow them:
blocks first, then components, then add to the system; no raw colors, fonts or react-native text/press
primitives in screens (lint enforces it); check light and dark in the in-app catalog (Sources → Design system).
